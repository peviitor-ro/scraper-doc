# 06 — Reference scrapers

The [peviitor-scrapers](https://github.com/peviitor-scrapers) organization is the canonical home for scrapers. New and active scrapers should live there so the team can maintain them if a volunteer steps away.

Older scrapers may also be found in [peviitor-ro](https://github.com/orgs/peviitor-ro/repositories?q=scraper) or personal accounts; treat those as historical unless a maintainer says otherwise.

Use these repos to learn how other people structured their scrapers, handled pagination, and uploaded jobs. Do not copy code blindly: careers pages change, and older scrapers may not follow the current [job model](https://github.com/peviitor-ro/peviitor_core#job-model-schema) or [best practices](15-best-practices.md).
