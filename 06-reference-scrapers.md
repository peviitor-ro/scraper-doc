# 06 — Reference scrapers

The [peviitor-scrapers](https://github.com/peviitor-scrapers) organization and the [scraper repositories in peviitor-ro](https://github.com/orgs/peviitor-ro/repositories?q=scraper) contain many examples.

Use them to learn how other people structured their scrapers, handled pagination, and uploaded jobs. Do not copy code blindly: careers pages change, and older scrapers may not follow the current [job model](https://github.com/peviitor-ro/peviitor_core#job-model-schema) or [best practices](15-best-practices.md).
