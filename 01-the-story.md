# 01 — The story

We want to build a scraper for a company so its open positions (jobs) appear in the [peviitor.ro](https://peviitor.ro) search engine.

A scraper reads the company's own careers page, extracts the minimum required data, and sends it to the [peviitor API](https://github.com/peviitor-ro/api). Volunteers can then check that the jobs are searchable and keep the scraper working when the careers page changes.
